Creating UserID
An IT Administrator will create a userID, link it to your email and send you the login details.
The administrator has a management console specifically for users in Helpdesk.
 
Here they will have a list of users that can be viewed, edited and deleted. They will also be able to assist you with resetting your password if you need assistance.
 

Logging In
To log in, simply click the log in button on the Helpdesk site and input your login details.
 

Forgotten Password
If you have forgotten your password, click the ‘Forgot your password?’ link.
You’ll be requested to input the email account associated with your userID.
 
Input your email and click ‘send reset link’ and you’ll shortly receive an email with a link. Ensure to check your junk/spam folder for the email.
The link is single use and expires one hour after it was issued. If you fail to reset the first time, you can simply request a new link, or contact an IT Administrator for assistance.
Input your new password twice in the password reset form, then you will be able to log in with the new password.

User Profile
Your user management tools are visible at the top right of Helpdesk.
 
From here you’ll be able to view and edit your profile with name details and phone number.
 
You can reset your password here if you feel it has been compromised.
The edit form also allows you to upload an avatar picture.
 

Tickets
Use the New Ticket button on the main tickets page to create a new ticket.
 
Give it a title and a description and save. You can then view the ticket contents. 
Ensure to give the ticket a descriptive title and put all the issue details in the description.
The created date can be back dated if need be. The default due date is 1-week from creation date, but can be adjusted if need be. The due date is a simple reminder for you in case a ticket has been forgotten about, or lost among other issues. Tickets will be highlighted red if they go overdue, for your convenience. The due date can be adjusted if more time is needed on an issue.
The outcome field is to summarise the outcome of the issue, along with the fixes that worked, as well as any products or additional equipment that may need to be invoiced to the customer.

Cases
Cases are a timeline within the ticket where you record every step along the way. Every attempted fix, correspondence with the customer, etc, are recorded in cases so that they can be referred to in detail later.
 
It is important to log cases in detail so that when other engineers pick up a ticket, they can appraise progress and don’t repeat troubleshooting steps you’ve already completed.
Cases are also used to record the amount of time spent on an issue. That time is used to bill service contract customers, or invoicing general customers labour hours.
Hours are recorded in increments of 15 minutes (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1). The timer can assist in keeping estimates accurate. All recorded time is rounded up to the nearest quarter.

Ticket Status
There are multiple states to a ticket.
 
Open: the ticket is active and being worked on.
Closed: the issue detailed in the ticket does not need addressing anymore, or at this time, so the ticket is closed. It can be re-opened as needed.
For Invoicing: The work outlined in the ticket has been completed, and accounts needs to invoice any additional labour/equipment to the customer before the ticket is closed.

Contacting IT Admin
The IT Administrator can be contacted along with all other engineers via email, phone or using the Helpdesk messenger app.
