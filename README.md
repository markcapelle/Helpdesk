## 🆔 Creating a UserID

An IT Administrator will create your userID, link it to your email, and send you the login details.

The administrator uses a dedicated management console within Helpdesk where they can:

- View users  
- Edit user details  
- Delete accounts  
- Assist with password resets  

---

## 🔐 Logging In

To log in, click the **Login** button on the Helpdesk site and enter your credentials.

---

## ❓ Forgotten Password

If you forget your password:

1. Click **“Forgot your password?”**  
2. Enter the email associated with your userID  
3. Click **Send reset link**

You will receive an email containing a password reset link.  
Check your **junk/spam** folder if it does not appear.

### Reset Link Details

- The link is **single‑use**  
- It expires after **one hour**  
- If it expires, simply request a new link  
- You may also contact an IT Administrator for assistance  

After opening the link, enter your new password twice to complete the reset.

## 🎫 Tickets

Use the **New Ticket** button on the main tickets page to create a ticket.

### Ticket Details

- Provide a **descriptive title**  
- Add all relevant issue details in the **description**  
- The **created date** can be back‑dated if needed  
- The **default due date** is one week from creation  
- Overdue tickets are highlighted **red**  
- The due date can be adjusted at any time  

### Outcome Field

Use the **Outcome** field to summarise:

- The final resolution  
- Fixes applied  
- Any products or equipment used  
- Items requiring invoicing  

---

## 🕒 Cases

Cases act as a **timeline** within a ticket, recording:

- Troubleshooting steps  
- Customer correspondence  
- Progress updates  

This ensures other engineers can quickly understand the ticket history without repeating work.

### Time Tracking

Cases also record time spent on an issue:

- Logged in **15‑minute increments**  
  - 0.25  
  - 0.5  
  - 0.75  
  - 1.0  
- The built‑in timer helps maintain accuracy  
- Time is always **rounded up** to the nearest quarter hour  

---

## 🏷️ Ticket Status

Tickets can be in one of several states:

### **Open**
The ticket is active and being worked on.

### **Closed**
The issue no longer requires attention.  
Closed tickets can be re‑opened if needed.

### **For Invoicing**
Work is complete and accounts must invoice labour/equipment before closing the ticket.
